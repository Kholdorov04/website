🏢 Our Story

Built by Gamers, for Gamers

We started in a garage with a shared passion for PC gaming. Today we serve 2.4 million players worldwide. Here's how we got here.

🎯 Our Mission

Making Great Games Accessible to All

We believe every gamer deserves access to the best titles at fair prices, with zero hassle. Our platform removes the barriers between you and your next favorite game — no region locks, no hidden fees, no complicated DRM.

From AAA blockbusters to indie masterpieces, we curate a library that reflects the full spectrum of PC gaming culture. We champion developers of all sizes and pass the savings directly to players.

💡 What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They guide every product decision, every hire, and every interaction with our community.

🎮

Gamer First

Every feature we build starts with one question: does this make the gaming experience better? We're gamers ourselves — we use the product daily and we hold it to the highest standard.

🤝

Radical Transparency

No hidden fees. No shady practices. We publish our pricing logic, our refund success rates, and our supply chain standards openly. We believe honesty builds loyalty.

🌱

Inclusive Community

Gaming is for everyone. We actively moderate our community to be welcoming and safe, regardless of skill level, background, or identity. No toxicity tolerated, period.

⚡

Speed of Innovation

The gaming industry moves fast. So do we. We ship new features weekly, respond to feedback within 24 hours, and adapt to player needs at startup speed, not enterprise pace.

🌍

Global Mindset

Great games know no borders. We serve players in 190+ countries with local payment methods, regional pricing, and multi-language support. Every gamer is a first-class citizen.

🔒

Security & Trust

Your account, your payment data, your gaming history — all protected with enterprise-grade security. We are GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2 Type II compliant.
