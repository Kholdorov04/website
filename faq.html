🔑 Game Keys & Delivery
Every key sold on PC Games Hub is sourced directly from publishers, distributors, or authorized resellers. All keys are verified, unused, and guaranteed to activate successfully. We have zero tolerance for gray-market keys and maintain strict supplier vetting. Every batch of keys is tested before being listed. In the extremely rare case a key doesn't work, contact support for an immediate replacement or refund.
Each game listing clearly shows its platform (Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, Battle.net, Origin/EA App, Ubisoft Connect, Xbox, etc.). Make sure you own an account on the correct platform before purchasing. Platform information is always displayed prominently on the product page and in your confirmation email.
Most keys are global/worldwide and work in any region. However, some publishers issue region-locked keys (e.g., "EU only" or "Americas only"). We clearly label all region restrictions on each product page. If you're in a different region than indicated, we recommend purchasing a globally unlocked key. Contact us before purchasing if you're unsure — we'll confirm compatibility for your specific region.

We offer a 30-day refund policy for games that are defective, incompatible with your hardware as advertised, or materially not as described. The key must not have been activated on any gaming platform. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method. For full details, see our Terms of Service.
Once a key is activated on a gaming platform (Steam, Epic, etc.), it cannot technically be "returned" as the game is now linked to your platform account. However, we evaluate these cases individually.
