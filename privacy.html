PC Games Hub ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at pcgameshub.com and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. Please read this policy carefully. By using our Service, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register, purchase, or interact with our Service, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, password (stored as a salted hash), and profile picture.
  • Purchase Information: Billing address, payment method details (processed by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partners — we never store raw card numbers), and transaction history.
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team, reviews you post, and community forum contributions.
  • Identity Verification: Where required by law or for fraud prevention, government-issued ID documents.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Service, we automatically collect:

  • Device & Browser Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, search queries, game library interactions, and navigation paths.
  • Location Data: Country and region inferred from IP address for regional pricing and compliance purposes.
  • Log Files: Server logs recording access times, referral URLs, and error codes for security and performance monitoring.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Login: If you sign in via Google, Steam, or Discord, we receive your public profile data from those services.
  • Payment Processors: Transaction confirmation and fraud-screening results from Stripe, PayPal, and other payment partners.
  • Analytics Partners: Aggregated behavioral insights from analytics providers to help us improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Processing purchases, delivering game keys, managing your library, and providing customer support.
  • Account Management: Creating and maintaining your account, authenticating your identity, and enabling account recovery.
  • Personalization: Powering our recommendation engine to surface games you're likely to enjoy based on your purchase history and ratings.
  • Communications: Sending transactional emails (receipts, key delivery), order updates, security alerts, and — with your consent — marketing communications and deal alerts.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraudulent transactions, unauthorized access, and other illegal activities.
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable laws including tax reporting, anti-money laundering regulations, and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Product Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns to improve features, fix bugs, and develop new capabilities.
  • Loyalty Program: Tracking points earned and redeemed through our rewards program.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who process data on our behalf (payment processors, cloud hosting, email delivery, fraud detection) under strict data processing agreements.
  • Publishers & Developers: Anonymized or aggregated purchase data shared with game publishers for royalty reporting. We never share personally identifiable information without your consent.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of PC Games Hub, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose with your explicit prior consent.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our Service:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication, session management, and security. Cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences (language, theme, region) to provide a personalized experience.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our Service (Google Analytics 4, with IP anonymization enabled).
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to serve relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness. Applied only with your explicit consent.

You can manage cookie preferences through our Cookie Settings panel or your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect Service functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account Data: Retained for the lifetime of your account, plus 30 days after deletion to allow recovery.
  • Transaction Records: Retained for 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations.
  • Support Communications: Retained for 3 years to provide context for future interactions.
  • Usage & Analytics Data: Anonymized after 26 months and retained in aggregate form indefinitely.
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, plus 90 days.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data where no overriding legal basis exists for retention.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling for marketing.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Non-Discrimination (CCPA): California residents will not be discriminated against for exercising their privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@pcgameshub.com or use the data request tool in your Account Settings. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner as required by law).

7. Data Security

We implement industry-leading security measures including:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • bcrypt password hashing with per-user salts
  • SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure
  • Regular third-party penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication for all internal systems
  • Intrusion detection and 24/7 security monitoring

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by GDPR, and comply with all applicable breach notification laws.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately at privacy@pcgameshub.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

PC Games Hub is headquartered in the United States. If you access our Service from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US or other countries. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for EU/EEA transfers
  • UK International Data Transfer Agreements for UK-origin data
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email (if you have an account) and post a prominent notice on our website at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, data requests, or to contact our Data Protection Officer:

  • Email: privacy@pcgameshub.com
  • Postal: PC Games Hub, Attn: Privacy Team, 123 Gaming Ave, San Francisco, CA 94102, USA
  • EU Representative: eu-privacy@pcgameshub.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the EU, you can find your national authority at edpb.europa.eu.
